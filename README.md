# 🌺 ಮಂಗಳೂರು ದಸರಾ ೨೦೨೬ · Mangaluru Dasara 2026
### Sri Gokarnanatha Kshetra, Kudroli | 3D Interactive Virtual Tour Experience

Experience "The Common Man's Dasara" (ಜನಸಾಮಾನ್ಯರ ದಸರಾ) in interactive 3D WebGL with your local coastal guide, **Kiran (ಕಿರಣ್)**.

---

## 🌟 Highlights & Features

- **🏛️ Sacred 3D Architecture**:
  - 4 Tamil-style Gopurams with seven-tiered Golden Gopuram & kalashas.
  - Sree Narayana Guru 1966 marble statue, footwear etiquette counter & green carpet walkway.
  - Sacred Pushkarini (temple pond) with 4 corner Shiva statues, water reflections, fountains, sculpted Mount Kailasa & Nandi.
  - 5 Historical Timeline Pillars (1912, 1966, 1991, 1995, 2026).
  - 2,400 instanced LED serial lights, 180 devotee figures, falling flower petals & aerial fireworks.

- **🪕 Sharada Mathe Darbar & Navadurgas (Since 1991)**:
  - Grand Sharada idol holding an ornate silver veena on a lotus peetha, circling aarti flame, rangoli & kaal-deepa oil lamps.
  - **Dynamic 2026 Saree Alankara**: Real-time Day 1 to Day 9 silk saree, blouse & garland color switcher.
  - Semicircular gallery with Mahaganapati and all 9 Navadurgas, celebrating Kudroli's distinctive tradition of worshipping the 7th form as **Mahakali**.

- **🐯 Vijayadashami Grand Procession & Tulunadu Spirit**:
  - Decorated Sharada Mathe procession chariot with pause/resume controls.
  - Leaping **Pili Vesha** (tiger dancers) and chende drummers.
  - Verified route map through Kudroli, Mannagudde, Ladyhill / Narayana Guru Circle, Lalbagh, PVS, Hampankatta, Car Street & Alake.

- **🎙️ Full Authentic Spoken Kannada & English Voice Narration**:
  - Complete native spoken Kannada voice audio clips for all 10 stops bundled in `audio/kn/`.
  - Articulated 3D guide "Kiran" with real-time lip-sync mouth animation, walking cycles, and cultural gestures (*Namaskara*, *Bow*, *Point*, *Wave*).
  - Web Audio soundscape with Sa-Pa drone, veena plucks, temple bells, conch shell, and procedural Chende & Tase percussion.

- **💡 Cultural Tools & Accessibility**:
  - 6 Bilingual Visitor Tips cards.
  - 8-Question Cultural Quiz with live scoring.
  - Full bilingual transcript.
  - Low Graphics & Reduced Motion modes.

## 💻 Local Development

Run locally using any static web server:

```bash
# Python
python -m http.server 8080

# Or Node.js
npx serve .
```

Then open `http://localhost:8080/index.html` in any web browser.
